Pinned posts never trigger the lower boundary. SWM stops after five consecutive non-pinned posts older than From.
Posts newer than To must be traversed to reach older material. Their traffic remains in WARC but they are excluded from the normalised CSV and JSONL.
Pinned posts are preserved but do not consume the N-post limit.
Comments are expensive. Facebook paginates comments separately for each post. This can add hundreds of requests, greatly increase runtime and raise the likelihood of throttling or verification.
SWM never asks for your Facebook password. Chrome retains its own session cookies in the dedicated local profile; SWM redacts Cookie/Authorization and Set-Cookie headers plus recognised login/session fields before writing WARC.
